# ADR-002: Checkout Application Service as BFF boundary

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-26

## Context

`OrderFacadeImpl` injects 12 sm-core services plus `CustomerFacade` and `ShoppingCartFacade`. Bypass APIs (`OrderPaymentApi`, `OrderTotalApi`, `OrderShippingApi`) inject additional services directly. This hub cannot move to microservices without a single orchestration entry point.

Onda 3 introduces `CheckoutApplicationService` as a monolith-local application service with DTO snapshots. Onda 6 makes it the **sole checkout boundary** when saga is enabled.

## Decision

`CheckoutApplicationService` lives in **`sm-shop`** (package `com.salesmanager.shop.checkout`) and owns:

- Place-order saga orchestration
- Tax computation (calls tax-service)
- Payment/shipping orchestration (calls integration-service)
- Cart clear after successful commit
- Totals/shipping quote endpoints delegated from bypass APIs

`OrderFacadeImpl` delegates checkout operations to this service; read operations delegate to `OrderFacadeHttpAdapter`.

## Alternatives considered

### Extract checkout to `checkout-service`

- **Pros:** Pure microservice boundary.
- **Cons:** Highest-risk wave adds third deployable; BFF still needs resolver and session context; doubles network hops for every checkout step.
- **Rejected for Wave 6** (AD-024 upgrade path).

### Keep OrderFacadeImpl as hub with HTTP clients

- **Pros:** Smaller refactor.
- **Cons:** 12 injections remain; untestable orchestration; violates HUB requirements.
- **Rejected.**

## Consequences

**Positive:** Testable saga; clear seam for future extraction; hub decomposition measurable (≤4 sm-core deps on checkout paths).

**Negative:** sm-shop grows in responsibility; must guard against new sm-core injections in facades (ArchUnit).
