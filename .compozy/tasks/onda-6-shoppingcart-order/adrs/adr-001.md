# ADR-001: One Compozy workflow for ShoppingCart + Order (Onda 6)

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-26

## Context

Onda 6 extracts the final two core commerce domains — shopping cart and order — in the same calendar window (master plan weeks 57–68). They share checkout orchestration, saga/outbox infrastructure, Strangler BFF wiring, and hard prerequisites from Ondas 3–5. Cart and order have a **bidirectional cycle** that must be broken atomically from an architecture perspective (totals API + saga).

TLC artifacts in `.specs/features/onda-6-shoppingcart-order/` define requirement IDs CART/ORD/CHK/HUB/STR and 62 sequenced tasks.

## Decision

Treat Onda 6 as **one Compozy workflow** (`onda-6-shoppingcart-order`) producing one PRD, one TechSpec, and task files covering `shoppingcart-service` and `order-service` plus BFF checkout boundary work. TLC in `.specs/` is authoritative; Compozy artifacts translate without scope change. Execute blocked until Ondas 3, 4, 5 complete.

## Alternatives considered

### Three workflows (cart, order, checkout)

- **Pros:** Smaller documents.
- **Cons:** Duplicates saga/Strangler decisions; obscures cycle-break dependency; checkout cannot ship without both services.
- **Rejected:** Cart totals and saga commit are cross-cutting.

### Extract order without cart

- **Pros:** Order is the hub.
- **Cons:** Leaves cart↔order cycle in monolith; cart still calls in-process OrderService.
- **Rejected:** Cycle break requires coordinated delivery (ADR-007).

## Consequences

**Positive:** Single backlog, unified flags, one Pact gate, aligned milestones (SC-ready, OR-read-ready, CHK-ready).

**Negative:** Large PRD/TechSpec; requires disciplined phasing.

**Risks:** Scope creep — mitigate with frozen non-goals from TLC spec.
