# ADR-001: One Compozy workflow for Catalog + Customer in Wave 4

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-07-26

## Context

Wave 4 of the Shopizer decomposition extracts catalog (read) and customer (profile) in the same calendar window (master plan weeks 39–48). Both domains share Strangler BFF configuration, Wave 3 contracts (`ProductSnapshot`, `CustomerSnapshot`), and cross-dependencies (cart merge needs customer snapshot while catalog feeds search indexing).

The master plan groups them in one wave. STATE.md AD-008 established the precedent of one TLC feature per multi-service wave (Onda 2).

## Decision

Treat Wave 4 as **one Compozy workflow** (`onda-4-catalog-customer`) producing one PRD, one TechSpec, and a task graph covering `catalog-service` (:8086) and `customer-service` (:8087) plus monolith Strangler adapters. TLC in `.specs/` is authoritative product/technical truth. Execute remains **blocked until Onda 3 completes**.

## Alternatives considered

### Alternative 1: Separate workflows per service

- **Pros:** Smaller documents; clearer ownership.
- **Cons:** Duplicates Strangler config, pact gates, and cart-merge/catalog-search coupling decisions.
- **Rejected:** Cross-deps and shared Wave 3 contracts favor one workflow.

### Alternative 2: Include shoppingcart-service in Wave 4

- **Pros:** Completes customer journey extraction.
- **Cons:** Cart has cycle with order (7/10 difficulty); master plan places cart in Onda 6.
- **Rejected:** Only decouple merge, do not extract cart.

### Alternative 3: Start Wave 4 before Wave 3

- **Pros:** Calendar parallelism.
- **Cons:** Without snapshots, catalog read and customer HTTP boundaries reintroduce MODEL coupling.
- **Rejected:** Hard prerequisite.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Single backlog, one `wave4.strangler` profile, unified pact gate.
- Aligns with Onda 2 packaging pattern.

### Negative

- Larger PRD/TechSpec than single-service waves.
- Two parallel tracks need CAT-ready / CUS-ready milestones.

### Risks

- Scope creep into catalog writes — mitigated by AD-006 and frozen non-goals.

## Implementation notes

- Artifacts live in `.compozy/tasks/onda-4-catalog-customer/`.
- Do not modify `.specs/` from Compozy without TLC review.
